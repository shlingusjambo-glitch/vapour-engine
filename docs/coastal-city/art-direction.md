# Port Solace: Mariner's Reach art direction

Mariner's Reach is an original working waterfront district in the fictional coastal American city of Port Solace. Its identity combines a weathered coastal hotel, a neighborhood provisions shop and a marine workshop. It is a compact environment for evaluating Vapour's renderer, imported assets and gameplay at human scale; it is not a finished AAA production.

## Public reference study

On September 5, 2026 the public [official Rockstar screenshot gallery](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media/screenshots) was inspected, including the Vice City 03 urban overview and adjacent beach and nighttime street imagery. No reference image, map, character, vehicle design, logo, audio, story or other Rockstar asset is included in this repository.

The following are artistic observations, not claims about Rockstar's implementation:

- The urban overview gains scale from a hierarchy of foreground street hardware, mid-rise buildings and distant towers. Recessed windows, balcony depth, rooftop equipment and vegetation break up large surfaces.
- Beach imagery places ordinary people and street objects against broad natural shapes. Consistent human scale and varied silhouettes matter more than the number of buildings.
- Night street imagery combines warm and cool practical lights with darker surrounding materials. Glowing signs remain accents rather than replacing the lighting of the scene.
- Distant buildings lose contrast; architecture near the camera retains clear material separation and fine edges.

These observations informed an independently authored kit: warm plaster, coral masonry, teal enamel, bronze frames, modeled palm leaflets and plausible road construction. Names and signage are original. The hotel and sedan are designed from generic dimensional constraints, not a traced source design.

## Quality gate

The kit uses real dimensions, manufactured edge radii, material regions, textured surfaces, detailed glazing and street furniture. The original Meridian sedan includes a shaped body shell, physically cut wheel wells, separate wheels with axle origins, mirrors, handles, lamp clusters and a basic interior.

Remaining art work is explicit: the kit has no finished realistic human character, sculpted face, cloth simulation, production animation, photogrammetric ground clutter, complete professional audio library or authored interiors beyond the vehicle. Procedural surface textures are original and repeatable, but do not replace a full scanned material library. Reflective vehicle glass is opaque until transparent material sorting is integrated and verified. Treat these as development assets and evaluate the actual Vapour runtime separately from the Blender asset proof render.
